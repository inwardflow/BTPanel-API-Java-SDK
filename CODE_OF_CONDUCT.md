# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming place for discussion and collaboration.

Examples of expected behavior:

- showing respect for different experiences and viewpoints
- giving feedback that is specific, kind, and actionable
- focusing on the technical problem instead of the person
- assuming good intent while still being honest about problems

## Unacceptable Behavior

- personal attacks or harassment
- discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- intimidation, threats, or deliberately disruptive conduct
- bad-faith spam, trolling, or sabotage of collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or moderate content that violates this code of conduct, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
